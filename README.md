PROJECT R.E.D. (Resilient Extraterrestrial Dwelling) What we developed: A 5-turn tactical simulator where players manage a Martian habitat by strategically investing limited technology across five core life-support systems (Energy, Air, Water, Food, and Protection) while keeping crew morale high. Guided by an AI robot named Marquitos, players navigate random environmental hazards—such as dust storms and solar flares—through interactive minigames and critical resource trade-offs. PROJECT R.E.D. makes real-world aerospace engineering trade-offs tangible and fun rather than overly simplified or overwhelming. Every mechanic is grounded in real space science, integrating NASA/CSA data on MOXIE oxygen extraction, 98% ISS water recycling, regolith perchlorate toxicity, DONKI space weather alerts, and OSDR biological research. It bridges complex space science and STEM education, empowering young learners to experience firsthand how interconnected decisions, resource management, and emergency responses dictate whether a mission succeeds or fails.

What does it do and how does it work?

PROJECT R.E.D. (Resilient Extraterrestrial Dwelling) is a 2D isometric turn-based management game where players take on the role of mission commander for an advanced human outpost on Mars.

Pre-Mission Configuration: Before landing, players allocate limited technology across five primary life-support systems (Energy, Air, Water, Food, and Protection), choosing to upgrade only two systems to maximum technology while leaving three at a baseline generic level.

Five-Turn Crisis Deck: Over five operational turns, the base encounters random environmental and internal emergencies drawn from an event deck (such as global dust storms, solar flares, CO₂ alarms, clogged water recyclers, or food mold).

Interactive Minigames: Emergencies trigger fast-paced minigames (balancing pressure bars, button sequences, or connecting wire circuits) to resolve active hazards.

Daily Nutrition & Morale: Players select three meals daily from a 9-item space menu (rehydratable, thermo-stabilized, and long-shelf-life items) to balance astronaut nutrition and maintain a hidden sixth system: Crew Morale.

AI Companion "Marquitos": An assistant robot accompanies the player, delivering real NASA/CSA space trivia, Earth updates, and post-mission evaluations.
